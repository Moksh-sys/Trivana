# 🪶 Trivana – Tribal Arts & Crafts Marketplace

> **“Where Heritage Meets the World.”**

Trivana is a web-based e-commerce platform designed to showcase and promote **tribal arts, traditional handicrafts, and handmade cultural products** through a modern digital marketplace.

The idea behind Trivana is not simply to build another online shopping website. It is to address the gap between **traditional tribal craftsmanship and modern digital consumers** by creating a dedicated platform where cultural products can be discovered, explored, and purchased through an e-commerce-style experience.

---

## 📌 Problem Statement

Traditional tribal art and handicrafts face several challenges in the modern market.

Many skilled artisans create unique handmade products, but their work often has **limited digital visibility and limited access to wider markets**. Customers who are interested in authentic tribal and folk products also find it difficult to discover different categories of traditional art in one place.

The major problems identified were:

### 1. Limited Digital Visibility
Many traditional and tribal art products are not easily discoverable through modern digital platforms.

### 2. Fragmented Marketplace
Tribal products such as pottery, paintings, jewellery, leather work, clothing, and home décor are often scattered across different platforms instead of being presented in one dedicated marketplace.

### 3. Difficulty Discovering Authentic Cultural Products
Customers interested in traditional crafts may struggle to find products based on specific categories and artistic styles.

### 4. Lack of Cultural Context
An art product is more than just an item for sale. Traditional crafts often represent **history, culture, creativity, and generations of knowledge**. Typical shopping interfaces do not always communicate this cultural value.

### 5. Traditional Craft Meets Modern Shopping
There is a need for a platform that combines the **identity of traditional craftsmanship with the convenience of a modern e-commerce interface**.

---

# 💡 Our Solution

**Trivana** was developed as a dedicated digital marketplace concept for tribal and traditional arts.

The platform brings different types of cultural products into a single online shopping experience while keeping the cultural identity of the products at the center.

Instead of treating tribal artwork as ordinary merchandise, Trivana attempts to create an experience where users can **discover categories, explore products, learn about traditional art, and interact with the marketplace through a simple interface.**

---

# 🎯 Project Objectives

The main objectives of Trivana are:

- Create a dedicated online marketplace for tribal and traditional arts.
- Improve digital visibility of handmade cultural products.
- Organize products into clear categories.
- Make traditional artwork easier for customers to discover.
- Combine cultural storytelling with an e-commerce-style experience.
- Provide a responsive interface for different screen sizes.
- Demonstrate how traditional commerce concepts can be represented through a modern web application.

The long-term vision for Trivana is to evolve from a frontend prototype into a complete **digital ecosystem for tribal artisans and cultural products**.
